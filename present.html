How to Submit

How to Submit a Paper

1

Choose a Conference

Visit the Upcoming Conferences page and choose the conference that matches your research field.

2

Create an Account & Register

Register as a participant through the conference portal. Fill in your personal and institutional details.

3

Submit Abstract

Upload your research abstract (150–300 words) in the required format through the online submission system.

4

Wait for Notification

The review team will send the acceptance decision within 2 weeks after the abstract submission deadline.

5

Submit Full Paper

If your abstract is accepted, submit your full paper in the required format for the peer review process.

6

Present & Publish

Present your work at the conference, and the publication process with partner journals begins after the conference.

📋 Submission Requirements

Abstract Format

150–300 words, in English, covering background, methods, results, and conclusions.

Full Paper Format

A Microsoft Word or LaTeX template is provided. Minimum 6 pages, maximum 20 pages.

Writing Language

English (preferred) or Indonesian for certain conferences.

Originality

Work must be original and previously unpublished. Similarity check via Turnitin (max. 25%).

Publication Opportunity

Academic Publication Opportunity

Your best work deserves global visibility through reputable indexed journals.

📗

Conference Proceedings

All presented papers will be published in ISBN conference proceedings and made available online.

🔍

Scopus-Indexed Journals

Selected top-quality papers receive a recommendation to submit to Scopus-indexed partner journals.

🌐

Web of Science

Some of our conferences partner with journals indexed in Web of Science (ESCI/SCIE/SSCI).

📖

DOAJ & Google Scholar

All proceedings and partner journals are listed in DOAJ and indexed by Google Scholar for maximum visibility.

✅

Double-Blind Peer Review

A rigorous review process by experienced reviewers to ensure the highest academic quality standards.

🔓

Open Access Options

Open access publication options are available so your research can be accessed by anyone worldwide.

Disclaimer: Publication in Scopus- or Web of Science-indexed journals is not guaranteed and depends on paper quality and each journal's editorial policy. Scholarvein facilitates recommendations but does not determine final journal acceptance decisions. Participants are encouraged to confirm publication details with the conference committee before registering.
