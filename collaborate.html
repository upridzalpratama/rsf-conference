Create an Impactful Conference

Bring Your Dream Academic Conference to Life

Do you have a vision to run an international academic conference? Scholarvein is your strategic partner, providing all the infrastructure you need, from the management system to a global participant network.

With our experience managing hundreds of conferences, you can focus on content and academic quality while we handle all the technical and operational aspects.

  • Ready-to-use paper submission & review platform
  • Access to a participant network from 120+ countries
  • Conference marketing & promotion support
  • International registration payment system
  • A professional support team throughout the conference
  • Publication options in Scholarvein partner indexed journals

Co-Organizer / Co-Host
Become a Co-Organizer / Co-Host

Become Part of the Organizing Team

Scholarvein's Co-Organizer program lets academic institutions, professional associations, or experienced individuals join as organizing partners for our international conferences.

As a Co-Organizer, you receive official credit as an organizer, access to our platform, and a share of the benefits from the conference's success.

  • Official credit as Co-Organizer / Co-Host
  • Your institution's logo on all conference promotional materials
  • Full access to the organizer dashboard
  • Revenue sharing from participant registrations
  • A broader academic collaboration network
  • Full technical support from the Scholarvein team

Become a Sponsor
Become a Sponsor

Support the Global Academic Ecosystem

Make your brand known to thousands of academics, researchers, and professionals worldwide. Sponsoring Scholarvein conferences gives you high visibility in an influential international academic community.

We offer a range of sponsorship packages that can be tailored to your organization's goals and budget, from brand awareness to lead generation among academics.

  • Sponsor logo on the website & all conference materials
  • A mention at the opening ceremony & on participant certificates
  • A virtual or physical booth in the networking area
  • Access to the participant database (subject to privacy policy)
  • A dedicated speaking slot for a sponsor representative
  • A post-conference reach & engagement report
