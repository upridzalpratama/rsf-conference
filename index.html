Academic Excellence

Publication & Academic Ecosystem

RSF Conference has built a robust academic ecosystem connecting your research with prestigious publication channels. Our conferences transform presentations into high-quality, peer-reviewed publications.

We collaborate with reputable indexed journals and conference proceedings recognized by leading academic databases, ensuring your work receives the global visibility it deserves. Each paper undergoes rigorous double-blind peer review by domain experts.

Our editorial team works closely with authors to ensure publication-ready quality, providing feedback and guidance throughout submission and revision.

Scopus Indexed Web of Science DOAJ Listed Google Scholar Double-Blind Peer Review Open Access Options

* Publication in indexed journals is subject to the quality of submitted papers and the specific conference's publication arrangement. Not all conferences guarantee Scopus or WoS indexing. Participants are encouraged to confirm publication details with the organizing committee prior to registration. RSF Conference facilitates but does not guarantee specific indexing outcomes.
